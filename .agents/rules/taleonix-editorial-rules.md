# Taleonix Publication & Story Generation Rules

## 1. Audience & Language Standard
- **Target Audience**: United States audience (70%+ US readers, 45–65 age group arriving from Facebook page networks).
- **Language**: Pristine, rich American English literary prose (never use Hindi/Hinglish in stories, titles, or reader-facing metadata).
- **Tone**: High-suspense, emotional drama, dramatic billionaire/mafia/family secrets, moral justice, and deep emotional stakes.

## 2. Story Structure & Novel-Grade Depth
- **Word Count & Length**: 18 to 25 long, detailed paragraphs per chapter (2,000–3,000+ words, 10–12 minute read time). Never write shallow summaries.
- **Atmospheric Formatting**:
  - Always include Location/Timeline header at the top (e.g. `[ OAKRIDGE AUDITORIUM, ATLANTA — 02:30 PM ]`).
  - Use realistic visceral dialogues, psychological tension, internal monologues, and intense emotional payoffs.
  - Include verified author bylines and clean dropcaps.

## 3. Strict Scene Imagery Rules
- **No Random Fillers**: Never throw generic stock or unrelated photos into the text.
- **100% Character & Scene Match**: Only use images where the characters, clothing, lighting, and environment 100% accurately match the exact story event.
- **Strategic Placement**: Insert 1 to 2 high-impact scene illustrations exactly at the narrative climax where that event is described.
- **Aspect Ratio**: Clean 16:9 widescreen cinematic photography or movie poster frames with descriptive captions.

## 4. Multi-Part Serialization & Seamless Navigation
- Sagas must have complete multi-part continuations:
  - Part 1 -> Part 2 -> Part 3 (Grand Finale).
- Dynamic continuation buttons:
  - Part 1: "🔥 READ CHAPTER 2 →"
  - Part 2: "👑 READ CHAPTER 3 (GRAND FINALE) →"
  - Finale: "🎉 SERIES CLIMAX COMPLETED"
- Always provide "← Read Previous Chapter" options (top quick banner and bottom card) for viewers arriving directly via social links.

## 5. AdSense & High-Scale Performance
- AdSense Publisher ID: `pub-3806896432302528` (Script in `<head>`, Meta tag, and `/ads.txt` permanently active).
- In-memory RAM caching for stories and analytics to support 1M+ views without database bottlenecks.
- Gzip/Brotli payload compression enabled.
- 24/7 self-ping keep-alive heartbeat active so Render instance never goes to sleep.
