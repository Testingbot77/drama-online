# WEB STORY ENGINE — 7-PAGE MICRO-DRAMA STORYTELLING BRIEFING

### 0. VIDEO ANALYSIS FIRST (Mandatory)
Analyze reel video frame by frame:
1. **THE PREMISE**: Relationship + violation in one line.
2. **THE CHARACTERS**: Locked character bible (approx age, Black American appearance, hairstyle, skin tone, build, wardrobe, accessories, baseline, relationships).
3. **THE SETTING**: Location bible (location, time, practical visual details).
4. **THE BEATS WITH TIMESTAMPS**: 0:00 hook, escalation, reveal, confrontation, climax, ending.
5. **THE DIALOGUE**: Every line + killer quote to reuse in confrontation.
6. **THE EVIDENCE OBJECT**: Physical item proving betrayal (envelope, keys, form, receipt, phone, coat, etc.).
7. **THE ENDING STATE**: Resolved/unresolved, open moral question.
8. **THE TONE**: Calm vs loud, composed vs performative.

*Rule: Never change what the video established. Add depth, inner thoughts, backstory, and aftermath without contradicting visual canon.*

### 1. CORE CONTRACT
- 30s reel = trailer. Web story = film.
- Pay the emotional debt immediately. Open with the unresolved wound.
- Drive the core debates: "Who is wrong?", "What would you do?", "Would you forgive this?"

### 2. THE 7-PAGE SKELETON (700–900 words per page; ~5,000–6,000 words total)
1. **Page 1: HOOK** — Open mid-conflict. Hook end: raw question (*"Who did this to her?"*).
2. **Page 2: RELATIONSHIP** — Flashback drip / warm memory turned sharp. Hook end: hidden wound (*"But I never told anyone what I saw that night."*).
3. **Page 3: EVIDENCE** — Control-symbol scene, object fought over/changing hands. Hook end: object's real meaning (*"That paper changed everything."*).
4. **Page 4: CONFRONTATION** — Face-to-face, short/interrupted dialogue, 1 killer line. Hook end: forces re-judgment (*"So he really did it."*).
5. **Page 5: TWIST** — New perspective/hidden motive flipping the reader's side. Hook end: reverse verdict (*"Wait — I was wrong about her."*).
6. **Page 6: CONSEQUENCE** — Aftermath, family silence, domestic weight. Hook end: quiet close (*"That night nobody ate dinner."*).
7. **Page 7: MORAL** — Boundary set, resolved-but-lingering. Hook end: comment bait (*"What would you do?"*).

### 3. THE PAGE-KEEPER & DIALOGUE LAWS
- **Last Line Hook**: Every single page ends on an irresistible cliffhook. Only Page 7 resolves.
- **Killer Lines**: One screenshot-worthy killer line per page. Calm, flat, restrained delivery beats shouting.
- **Evidence Object**: Physical token fought over and changing hands.
- **Content Boundaries**: No graphic harm, strict protection around child/grief dynamics, no melodrama/caricatures. Authentic, restrained Black American family realism.
